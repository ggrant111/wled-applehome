Restart bridge

Restart to publish added or removed lights, grouped effects, and renamed presets to Apple Home. This temporarily interrupts responses, but does not erase pairing.

Firmware updates

Update over HomeSpan’s password-protected ArduinoOTA service. The browser dashboard does not upload firmware directly.

Important: Download the binary for your exact board, use its network OTA port and configured admin password, and never erase ESP32 NVS or repartition the flash.
Open firmware builds ↗
Security: This local HTTP dashboard uses password-based Digest authentication and a per-boot CSRF token. Keep port 8080 and OTA available only on your trusted LAN; do not publish either service to the internet.
